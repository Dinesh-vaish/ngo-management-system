## ⚡ Why does the world need SQ NGO? (The Real-World Problems We Solve)

Traditional software is built for air-conditioned offices. **SQ NGO is built for the rugged Indian terrains.** Here is how we tackle the biggest pain points in the NGO sector:

### 📶 1. The "No Internet in Villages" Problem
* **The Pain:** 70% of ground-level NGO work happens in rural areas with zero connectivity. Volunteers have to carry physical registers.
* **Our Killer Solution:** Built as an **Offline-First PWA**. Field workers can register beneficiaries and take attendance in the middle of a jungle. The moment they get a network, data auto-syncs securely!

### 🛑 2. The "Trust & FCRA Regulation" Nightmare
* **The Pain:** Strict government compliance and FCRA rules mean one small accounting mistake can shut down an NGO. Donors demand 100% proof of where their money went.
* **Our Killer Solution:** Real-time **Transparency Portals** and bulletproof budget variations. Every single expense is tied to an approval workflow, keeping the NGO 100% clean and compliant.

### 🕵️‍♂️ 3. The "Ghost Beneficiaries" Fraud
* **The Pain:** Lack of verification leads to fake entries, destroying donor trust.
* **Our Killer Solution:** **GPS & QR-Based Check-ins**. Volunteers and distribution camps are tracked via geo-fencing (50m-500m radius). No more fake data.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

We don't just write code; we design scalable systems:
* **Frontend:** PWA-ready HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (with IndexedDB for local offline caching)
* **Backend:** Scalable REST APIs designed for multi-tenant isolation.
* **Database:** Relational schema optimized with `Tenant_ID` scoping for 100% data privacy between multiple NGOs.
